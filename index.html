How to play

  • Move — WASD / arrow keys, or the stick on the left of the screen.
  • Dig — stand at a hay pile and hold the action button (or Space).
  • Sift — carry your load to the conveyor in the corner and tip it in. Sifted hay pays coins.
  • The needle is buried at some depth in one pile. Dig that pile down far enough and you will see it glinting in what is left — then walk over and grab it.
  • Digging the wrong pile is never wasted — it still pays. Needle Sense tells you how far off the needle is from where you stand, so take a reading, walk somewhere else, take another, and close in.
  • Spend coins on shovels and gear. Better shovels unlock as you clear barns.
  • The Daily Barn is the same barn for everybody, every day: fixed kit, no coins, no shop — just the clock. Come back tomorrow for a new one and keep your run going.
  • Odds and ends come out of the hay too. Every kind you dig up goes on the Barn Shelf — fill it and there is a bounty and a hat in it.
  • From barn 8 you can retire the farm: you hand back the coins, the shovels and the gear and start at barn 1 again, but you keep a rosette for every needle you found. Each one is +10% coins and +5% digging for good, and every eight of them lets you buy the shovels a barn earlier. Your farmer and your Barn Shelf come with you.
